## Case 014 — The Date That Lied

**Mechanic:** stale information

**Temporal frame:** August 28, 2026
**Unsupported claim:** the live-action *Legend of Zelda* film opens May 7, 2027.

Why it works: Sony's fiscal-year schedule genuinely listed May 7, which makes the claim well sourced rather than invented. Nintendo later moved the worldwide opening to April 30, 2027. The player must search after the authentic document.

## Case 027 — The Echo Chamber

**Mechanic:** circular sourcing

**Temporal frame:** December 16, 2024
**Unsupported claim:** a *Barbie* sequel was officially in early development with Greta Gerwig and Noah Baumbach.

Why it works: the story appeared across many publications, but those stories traced to one report. Representatives for both filmmakers directly disputed it. The case is intentionally frozen to December 16, 2024; later franchise negotiations are not retroactive evidence.

Primary and corroborating sources:
